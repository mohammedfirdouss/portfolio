SmartKey is a digital key-management system for the University of Lagos Senate Building. It replaces a paper logbook with real-time tracking, role-based access, and an immutable audit trail for every key in the building.

## What it is

Staff request a key, collect it at the security desk with a one-time code, and return it the same way — every step logged, timestamped, and auditable. Four role-specific dashboards (Chief Security Officer, Dean, Verifier, Requester) cover the full lifecycle, and three AI components — rule-based risk scoring, Gemini-generated shift reports, and pixel-level signature verification — flag anomalies that paper could never catch.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and Supabase (Postgres with Row-Level Security), with the Google Gemini API for AI features.
