Preparing a khutbah means finding the right ayat, getting every reference right, and shaping it all into a structure. Bayan helps with the research: it searches the Quran by meaning, checks verse references, and suggests an outline from a topic.

It is meant to assist, not replace. It does not write the khutbah, so the words and the reasoning stay the khateeb's own.
