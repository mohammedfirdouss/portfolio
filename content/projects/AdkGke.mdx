Give it a company name or a stock ticker, and it returns an investment report with a bull case and a bear case.

## How the agents work together

A researcher gathers background on the company. An analyst turns it into an investment thesis, and a critic pushes back. They go around again until the critic accepts the thesis or three rounds have passed. The limit keeps the loop from running forever. Then two writers take the final thesis and write the bull and bear cases at the same time.

In ADK terms, the root is a `SequentialAgent` and the research team is a `LoopAgent`. The thesis moves between agents through shared state: `PROMPT` → `research` → `INVESTMENT_THESIS`.

Built with Google ADK 1.27.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, LangChain, FastAPI and Uvicorn, running on GKE Autopilot.
