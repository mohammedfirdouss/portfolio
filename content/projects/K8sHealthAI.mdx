When a pod fails, someone has to collect its events, spec and logs by hand, usually under pressure. K8s Health AI does that work instead. It watches for failing workloads, gathers the context, asks an AI backend for a diagnosis, and writes the result into a `ClusterDiagnosis` custom resource the whole team can see.

The backend can be OpenAI, Bedrock, Vertex, Azure OpenAI, Ollama, or a mock for testing. A Prometheus metric (`diagnoses_total`) and a small CLI (`diagctl`) cover day-to-day operations.

Built in Go with controller-runtime.
