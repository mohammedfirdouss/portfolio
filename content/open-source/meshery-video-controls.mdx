On large screens, the video modals on meshery.io (the site for the CNCF Meshery project) pushed the player's controls out of view.

I centered the modal overlay with flexbox across the full viewport, capped its height so it scrolls internally, and made the video scale to the modal width with a max height. The native controls now stay visible at every screen size.
