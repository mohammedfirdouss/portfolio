I refactored `InternalEventMiddleware` in GitLab AI Gateway. I moved it out of a crowded base module into its own middleware file, and moved its tests with it.

This was part of a wider cleanup to make middleware easier to find, test, and maintain, without breaking existing imports.

What I shipped in MR !2291:

- moved the middleware into `ai_gateway/api/middleware/internal_event.py`,
- split and updated the tests in the middleware test module,
- kept old imports working by re-exporting where needed.

During review, maintainers flagged a circular import and some logger-related test failures. I fixed both, and the MR merged after final approval.
