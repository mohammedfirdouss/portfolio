I created the blog contribution guide for PipeCD so community members have a clear path to propose and publish technical posts.

The guide covers what kind of content is expected, the frontmatter and format rules, and how submission and review work.

I also made the docs structure match across `docs-dev` and `docs-v1.0.x`, including a section rename to keep the contribution docs consistent.

This was part of the wider contributor-docs effort in issue #6124, and it merged after maintainer review.
