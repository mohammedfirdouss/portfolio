*   Enabled PipeCD to roll out changes step by step across multiple Kubernetes clusters by building the full `kubernetes_multicluster` plugin, with six production pipeline stages: canary rollout, baseline rollout, primary rollout, traffic routing, canary clean, and baseline clean.
*   Removed the risk of cleanup touching production workloads by isolating resources with `pipecd.dev/variant` labels, so canary and baseline variants can be cleaned up independently.
*   Sped up multi-cluster operations by running them in parallel with Go's `errgroup` pattern, and made traffic routing fully visible with per-route logging.
